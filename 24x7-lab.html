24x7 Pathology Laboratory in Greater Kailash

A physical, staffed laboratory you can visit at night, on Sundays and on public holidays.

The laboratory at S-35, Greater Kailash Part 1 is open 24x7x365. It supports walk-in sample collection for tests that can be accepted safely at that time and for which the patient has prepared correctly.

FNAC, semen analysis and some procedure-dependent services require advance scheduling. “Open 24x7” should not be interpreted as every test or procedure being immediately available at every hour.

Patients can call before travelling to confirm preparation, sample requirements, current price and expected report time.

Frequently asked

Is every test available at 2 AM?

No. Collection depends on patient preparation, sample stability, staffing and whether the service requires scheduling.

Is the centre open on Sunday?

Yes, the main centre is open on Sundays and public holidays.

Is this an emergency service?

No. The laboratory performs pathology testing and is not a substitute for an emergency department.
