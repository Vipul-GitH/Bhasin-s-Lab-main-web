Hospital, Clinic & Institutional Services

Institutional relationships need operational detail, flexibility and accountability—not brochure claims.

The laboratory is interested in hospitals, nursing homes, IVF centres, clinics, corporates, embassies, insurers and other institutions requiring a structured pathology relationship.

Potential models include hospital laboratory management, doctor-clinic services, pre-employment testing, annual health checks, onsite collection and tailored institutional logistics.

Discounts, service levels, turnaround targets and geographic commitments are discussed privately after the requirement is qualified. The website should not publish confidential or blanket commercial promises.
