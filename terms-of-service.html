1. General information

This website provides general information about Dr Bhasin’s Lab, its locations and selected service pathways. Website content does not create a doctor–patient relationship and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional.

2. Medical information

Preparation, sample, method, turnaround, reference interval and interpretation can vary by test and clinical context. Confirm current information directly with the laboratory and discuss clinical decisions with the treating doctor.

The website and pathology laboratory are not emergency medical services. People with severe or rapidly worsening symptoms should seek appropriate emergency medical care.

NABL accreditation applies to the accredited facility and the specific tests and methods listed in the current scope. The presence of an accreditation reference on a general page must not be interpreted as accreditation of every service offered.
