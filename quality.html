Accreditation & Quality

NABL accreditation is evidence of a defined quality system and assessed scope—not a blanket badge for every test.

The accredited permanent facility is Dr Bhasin’s Lab, S-35, Greater Kailash Part 1, New Delhi, operated by Dr. P. Bhasin Pathlabs Private Limited.

The current certificate is NABLM0626DL08110 under ISO 15189:2022, valid from 17 May 2026 to 16 May 2030. The detailed scope identifies the specific tests and methods assessed by NABL.

The scope includes disciplines represented under Clinical Biochemistry, Clinical Pathology, Cytopathology, Haematology & Immunohaematology, Histopathology, Microbiology & Infectious Disease Serology and Molecular Diagnostics. Only the listed tests and methods are accredited.

Internal quality control

Routine control material helps assess whether analytical performance remains within defined limits.

External quality assessment

Calibration and maintenance

Analysers and measurement systems require scheduled calibration, verification and maintenance.

Sample rejection criteria

Unsuitable or incorrectly identified samples must be recognised before unreliable results are produced.

Frequently asked

Does NABL accreditation mean every test is accredited?

No. Accreditation applies to the specific tests and methods in the current scope.

Can I download the certificate and scope?

Yes. The final site should provide both documents.

Does accreditation guarantee that no error can ever occur?

No. Accreditation assesses systems, competence and defined processes; it is not an absolute error-free guarantee.
