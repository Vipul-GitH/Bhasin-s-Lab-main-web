Privacy Notice — Draft

A practical draft for website enquiries and CRM submissions. Legal and data-protection review is required before publication.

Draft status: This notice is not a substitute for legal review. It reflects the current Phase 1 plan in which website enquiries may be transferred to the laboratory CRM and retained for up to one year.

1. Information collected

The website may collect information that you voluntarily enter in a form, such as your name, telephone number, email address, locality, enquiry type and message. Do not submit unnecessary medical records or sensitive information through a general enquiry form.

2. Why the information is used

Information may be used to respond to a request, arrange a callback, discuss home collection or centre services, support a doctor or institutional enquiry, and maintain an operational record of the interaction.

3. Sharing and service providers

Website submissions may be sent to the laboratory’s CRM and to authorised staff or service providers who support the website and communication workflow. The final production notice must identify applicable third parties and contractual safeguards.

4. Retention

Management has proposed retaining web-form submissions for one year. This period must be reviewed against the final purpose, legal obligations and CRM configuration.

5. Cookies and analytics

Only strictly necessary cookies should operate by default. Non-essential analytics or advertising cookies should be activated only through an appropriate consent mechanism and disclosed in the final cookie notice.

6. Security and limitations

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be applied, including HTTPS, access control, secure hosting, form protection and data minimisation. No internet transmission can be described as absolutely secure.

7. Your requests

Requests regarding access, correction or deletion should be sent to the published contact channel, subject to identity verification and applicable legal or clinical-record requirements.
