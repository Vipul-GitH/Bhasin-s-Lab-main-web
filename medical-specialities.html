Medical Speciality Support

The website should generate a qualified conversation—not overwhelm doctors with an unverified catalogue.

Management identifies gynaecologists, IVF specialists, physicians, medical oncologists, surgeons, dermatologists, urologists and gastroenterologists as important referral groups.

At launch, the page invites doctors to share their speciality, organisation, location and clinical-testing needs. A laboratory representative can then provide current methods, sample requirements, turnaround and commercial information.

Test-level claims, urgent-report promises and accreditation badges should be added only after verification against the current catalogue and NABL scope.

Gynaecology & IVF

Hormonal, reproductive and selected specialised testing pathways.

Internal medicine

Routine and specialised laboratory support for ongoing patient care.

Oncology

Testing pathways requiring careful sample, method and clinical coordination.

Surgery & hospital care

Pre-operative, inpatient and follow-up laboratory requirements.

How the service works

01

Share the requirement

Complete the doctor enquiry form or call the laboratory.

02

Qualify the need

The team confirms speciality, test need, expected volume, pickup location and preferred contact.

03

Meet the representative

Current methods, sample logistics, reporting and commercial terms are discussed.

04

Start with agreed workflow

Containers, pickup, reporting access and escalation routes are documented.

05

Review the relationship

Operational and clinical issues are followed up through the assigned team.

Service information

  • Launch approach: Enquiry-led
  • Detailed menus: Phase 2 after verification
  • Doctor login: Not included at Phase 1
  • Response owner: CRM / relationship team
