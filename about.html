About Dr Bhasin’s Lab

An independent, family-owned and medically led pathology laboratory serving patients and referring doctors from Greater Kailash I since 2000.

Our story

Built around a simple belief: the patient comes first.

Dr Bhasin’s Lab was founded on 23 January 2000 by Dr Pramod Bhasin and Mrs Arti Bhasin.

The founders wanted to combine dependable laboratory reporting with patient-friendly service at a time when automation and accessible technology were not common across independent laboratories.

The laboratory began as a 24-hour service at S-13, Greater Kailash Part 1 and is now led by a family of medical professionals through Dr. P. Bhasin Pathlabs Private Limited.
