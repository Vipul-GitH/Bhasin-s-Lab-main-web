Paediatric Sample Collection

A child-aware pathology collection experience at the GK-I centre and through home collection, with realistic expectations and no promise of a painless or single-prick draw.

Paediatric Sample Collection

Children need time, familiarity and calm communication—not a rushed collection.

The main centre includes a paediatric area with toys and space for children to become familiar with the environment. Staff can use reclining collection chairs and conversation to reduce anxiety.

No website should promise a painless or single-prick collection. Difficult veins and child anxiety are handled by assessing the situation carefully and seeking assistance rather than repeatedly attempting without confidence.

Parents should confirm fasting, medicine timing, sample type and any age-specific preparation before arriving.

Child-friendly space

Toys and an unhurried environment help children settle.

Calm communication

Staff engage the child and parent throughout the visit.

Reclining collection

Specialised chairs can help patients who feel faint or anxious.

Home option

Home collection may be more comfortable for some families.

Frequently asked

Can you guarantee one prick?

No. The team will assess carefully and may seek another experienced phlebotomist when required.

Should I tell my child in advance?

Age-appropriate honest preparation is usually better than surprising the child. Avoid threatening or fear-based language.

Can the sample be collected at home?

Yes, subject to serviceability and the sample required.
