For Referring Doctors

The first objective is a useful conversation with the right laboratory team.

The Phase 1 doctor pathway does not publish a static technical catalogue that could become outdated. It captures the doctor’s speciality, organisation, location, testing need and preferred contact.

A representative can provide current methods, sample requirements, turnaround information, pickup options, report access and commercial terms after understanding the clinical and operational requirement.

Technical or accreditation claims must be verified before being placed on individual speciality or test pages.

Medical relationship

A named team follows the enquiry rather than directing the doctor through a generic marketplace.

Clinic logistics

Pickup routes, containers, sample handling and reporting are discussed for the actual need.

Reporting support

Access, communication and escalation pathways are agreed operationally.

Multiple engagement models

Clinic services, institutional testing and hospital laboratory management can be discussed.
