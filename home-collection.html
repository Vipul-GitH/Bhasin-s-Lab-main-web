Who it suits

Senior citizens

If getting to a laboratory is tiring or difficult for an older family member, let the lab come to them. Dr Bhasin's Lab offers a blood test at home for senior citizens, with the same care you would get at our laboratory.

Busy professionals

Work should not decide when you get tested. Book an early-morning visit from 6 AM, before your day starts, or a late-evening visit after work. The last visit is at midnight. You can book at any hour, and for fasting tests a 6 AM visit means you are done before breakfast.

Patients who prefer to stay away from crowded places

If you live with a long-term condition, or your doctor has advised you to limit time in crowded places, a home visit lets you skip the waiting room entirely. Your doctor decides which tests you need and how often. We make the collection easy and comfortable.
