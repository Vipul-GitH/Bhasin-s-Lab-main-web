Who can use Ayushman CAPF here

Serving personnel and their eligible dependants from these eight forces: Assam Rifles (AR), Border Security Force (BSF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), National Security Guard (NSG) and National Disaster Response Force (NDRF).

Under the scheme, diagnostics at CGHS-empanelled centres signed up with the National Health Authority are cashless at CGHS rates. At our lab, please bring your CGHS card as well as your activated CAPF card, printed or on your phone, and a referral from a CAPF hospital, a government hospital or a CGHS wellness centre. Dependants over 75 do not need a referral.

CGHS

Secure OTP and geotagged photo

A routine CGHS step. We handle it for you.

For CGHS OTP verification, we call only from 011-49989898. CGHS sends a one-time password to your registered mobile, and you read it out to us. The code lasts only a few minutes, so we ask for it at the time of your test, never days before.

Our authorised staff take the required geotagged photograph during the home or centre collection process.

  • At home or at our lab, wherever your sample is collected.
  • The photo is taken by our authorised staff as part of the collection visit.
  • The photo is geotagged (it records the location), as CGHS requires.

If your own phone is not to hand, CGHS allows an attendant's mobile number in exceptional cases; just ask us. And if you ever get a CGHS message for a test you did not have, tell the CGHS Additional Director's office where your card is registered.

We call only from 011-49989898. Never share your OTP with anyone else.

These are CGHS steps. For CAPF, we follow whatever that scheme asks for.

What should I photograph and send?

Take a clear photo of these details and send it to us. We will tell you if anything else is needed. Lay the card or paper flat, use good light and keep all edges in view.

CGHS card

  • Name, as printed on the card
  • Beneficiary ID, with every digit readable
  • Category, for example pensioner or serving (green strip for pensioners, blue for serving employees)
  • Validity: the valid-till date must show

CAPF card

  • Name, as printed on the card
  • Beneficiary ID, with every digit readable
  • Category: force, and self or dependant
  • Validity date, if one is printed

Prescription or referral

  • Date, clearly readable
  • Patient name and ID, which should match the card
  • Tests advised, with every test clearly listed
  • Doctor's signature, and the stamp if there is one

The illustrated examples are fictional samples. Any photographed card or referral is a real document with the patient's details blurred.

Common questions

Is Dr Bhasin's Lab CGHS empanelled?

Yes. We have been on the CGHS panel for over two decades, and we are listed in the latest CGHS Delhi and NCR empanelment order. You will find us at S-35, Hansraj Gupta Road, Greater Kailash-I, New Delhi 110048.

Can I get CGHS or Ayushman CAPF blood tests done at home?

Yes. Our team collects samples at home every day from 6 AM to midnight, across South Delhi and the rest of Delhi and NCR. Send us your card and your prescription or referral first, and we will check them before we book your visit.

Is home collection free?

Yes, for bills above a nominal amount. Home collection is our own service rather than a government benefit, and our team will tell you how it applies when you book. Printed report delivery is free, with no minimum bill.

Do I need a CGHS referral or prescription for lab tests?

Yes, in most cases. At a standalone lab like ours, CGHS tests need a referral from your CGHS wellness centre or a government doctor, and some higher-value tests also need a CGHS endorsement. Send us what you have, and we will tell you before collection if anything else is needed.

What should I send before home collection?

A clear photo of your card and your prescription or referral. A digital CGHS card is fine, as CGHS accepts an e-card from the CGHS website, the myCGHS app or DigiLocker, and a downloaded prescription or e-referral works just as well for our check. On the day, keep your registered mobile phone nearby.

Can CGHS pensioners get tests cashless?

Yes. CGHS pensioners with a green-strip card are among the beneficiaries who always receive cashless treatment, and we offer cashless service to every eligible CGHS beneficiary. Send your card and referral first, and we will confirm everything before collection.

I'm a serving Central Government employee. Will my test be cashless, or do I pay CGHS rates?

Most serving employees pay at CGHS rates and claim the amount back from their department. Serving staff of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, CGHS and DGHS, and their dependants, can be cashless. Send us your card and we will tell you which applies to you.

I'm over 70. Do I still need a referral, and is it cashless?

Yes, a standalone lab like ours still needs a referral or prescription, even though you can now see a specialist directly. CGHS asks for a referral from a CGHS or government doctor, or a prescription issued by an empanelled hospital. Pensioners remain cashless, serving dependants pay and claim it back, and the OTP and geotagged photo still apply.

Why do you ask for an OTP, and which number will call me?

CGHS needs an OTP for every cashless claim, and we will only ever call you for it from 011-49989898. The code arrives on your registered mobile and lasts a few minutes, so we ask for it at the time of your test. If anyone calls from a different number asking for a CGHS OTP, do not share it, and call us on 93111 93111.

Why do you take a geotagged photo, and can it be done at home?

CGHS asks for it, and yes, we take it at your home when we collect there. CGHS has told empanelled centres to capture a geotagged photograph at the point of care, which records where and when your test took place. It takes a moment, and it helps protect your card from misuse.

Which tests are charged at CGHS rates, and where can I see the rate list?

Every test on the CGHS rate list that we do in-house is charged at the CGHS rate. Tests outside that list are charged at our own rates, and we tell you before collection. The current official rates apply from 13 October 2025, and you can open the CGHS rates document from this page.

Who can use Ayushman CAPF here?

Serving personnel and their eligible dependants from eight forces. They are Assam Rifles, the Border Security Force (BSF), Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), Central Industrial Security Force (CISF), Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP), Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB), National Security Guard (NSG) and National Disaster Response Force (NDRF). Ayushman CAPF is run by the Ministry of Home Affairs with the National Health Authority.

Can Ayushman CAPF beneficiaries get cashless lab tests?

Yes, the scheme offers cashless diagnostics at CGHS rates in CGHS-empanelled centres that are signed up with the National Health Authority. You need an activated Ayushman CAPF card and a referral from a CAPF hospital, a government hospital or a CGHS wellness centre. Send us both, and we will confirm how your test will be billed before collection.

Do I need both a CGHS card and an Ayushman CAPF card?

Yes. For Ayushman CAPF tests at our lab, please keep both your CGHS card and your activated Ayushman CAPF card ready, along with your referral. Send us photos of all three on WhatsApp to 93111 93111, and we will check them before collection.
