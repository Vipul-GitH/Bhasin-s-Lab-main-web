Photography direction

Approachable, accountable and real

Use a consistent portrait system: medium shots, soft natural or diffused light, neutral laboratory background, relaxed professional expression and direct eye contact where appropriate.

Environmental working images should show report review, quality discussion and real laboratory coordination. Do not stage doctors staring into microscopes when that is not the actual task.

Photography brief Group environmental portrait of the five medical professionals in the real laboratory. Balanced spacing, clean branded background, no patient data, no crossed-arm “corporate wall” pose. Produce both horizontal and vertical crops.
