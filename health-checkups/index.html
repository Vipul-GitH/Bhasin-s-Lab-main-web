COMMON QUESTIONS

Health checkup questions

Which health checkup packages does Dr Bhasin's Lab offer?+

Dr Bhasin's Lab, an independent, doctor-led pathology laboratory at S-35, Greater Kailash-I, South Delhi, offers five health checkup packages: Sehat Start Package (45 tests, Rs 1,300), Basic Health Check Up (76 tests, Rs 1,800), Advance Health Check Up (85 tests, Rs 2,800), Premium Health Check Up (91 tests, Rs 5,200) and Cancer Marker Package (10 tests, Rs 5,000). The Basic, Advance and Premium packages include all tests in the Sehat Start Package. Health checkup packages need fasting, at least 8 hours for Sehat Start and at least 10 hours for the others, and the Cancer Marker Package does not need fasting. Reports for the four main packages are ready in 8 hours. In the Cancer Marker Package, protein electrophoresis and CA 72-4 are reported on the third day and the other tests the same day. Each package section lists the tests included. The laboratory is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year, for walk-in sample collection. To book or ask which package suits you, call or WhatsApp 93111 93111.

How long does it take to get the report?+

Reports for the Sehat Start, Basic, Advance and Premium packages are ready in 8 hours, with no sample cut-off time. For the Cancer Marker Package, protein electrophoresis and CA 72-4 are reported on the third day after sample collection, and the other tests are reported the same day. When you book, we will tell you how you will receive your report.

How much does a health checkup cost?+

Current prices are Rs 1,300 for the Sehat Start Package, Rs 1,800 for the Basic Health Check Up, Rs 2,800 for the Advance Health Check Up, Rs 5,200 for the Premium Health Check Up and Rs 5,000 for the Cancer Marker Package. Please call 93111 93111 to confirm the current price before you visit.
