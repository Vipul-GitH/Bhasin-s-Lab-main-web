Semen Analysis & Semen DFI

Privacy, correct preparation and accurate timing matter as much as the laboratory process.

Semen analysis and Semen DNA Fragmentation Index testing are scheduled services rather than unrestricted 24x7 walk-ins. The final appointment time, preparation and abstinence instructions must be confirmed directly with the laboratory.

The main centre has a private semen-collection room. Patient identity, collection time, completeness of the sample and transport conditions are important pre-analytical details.

Test results should be interpreted by the treating clinician in the context of the couple’s history and other fertility investigations. A single result may not answer every fertility question.

Private collection room

A dedicated room supports privacy and correct on-site collection.

Scheduled service

Appointment and preparation are confirmed before arrival.

Time-sensitive handling

Collection time and prompt laboratory handling are recorded.

Clinical context

Reports should be reviewed with the treating fertility specialist or urologist.

Frequently asked

Can I walk in at any time?

No. Semen analysis and DFI require scheduling.

Can the result diagnose infertility by itself?

No. Fertility assessment is clinical and often requires multiple investigations.

What preparation is required?

The exact preparation depends on the requested test and must be confirmed at booking.
